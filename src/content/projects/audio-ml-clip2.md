## Listening through a model
These independent experiments use the Cadenza CLIP2 baseline and dataset to examine audio representations, regression models and hearing-loss severity information.

## Lines of inquiry
The work explores how audio can be represented for regression and how hearing-loss severity information can be included in that investigation.
