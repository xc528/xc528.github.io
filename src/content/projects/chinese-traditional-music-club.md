## Music as a shared space
I co-founded and served as president of NYU’s Chinese Traditional Music Club. The organization produced and participated in concerts, cultural showcases and collaborative performances.

## From programming to performance
My work brought together event coordination, music programming, team leadership, communication and concert production. Collaboration and community engagement were central to the organization’s work.

## A collective practice
The club brought people together through traditional Chinese music and collaborative performance. This experience connects my work as a musician with the organizational side of presenting music to a community.
