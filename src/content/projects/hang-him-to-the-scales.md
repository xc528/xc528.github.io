## A meeting of musical worlds
I am a member of Hang Him to the Scales, an indie / dreampop / shoegaze band incorporating Chinese musical elements. My contributions bring together performance and audio finishing.

## My contribution
I am the mastering engineer and perform pipa and bass guitar in the band. These roles connect my work as an engineer with my experience as a performing musician.
