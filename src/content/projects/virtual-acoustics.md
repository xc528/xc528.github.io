## The question
A machine learning model can learn from sounds placed inside simulated rooms. But the simulation changes the training material: reflections and reverberation affect how a sound reaches a listener or microphone. My thesis investigates how the choice of virtual acoustics method affects sound event localization and detection model performance.

## Thesis
*Exploring Virtual Acoustics Algorithms in the SpatialScaper Framework: Effects on Sound Event Localization Model Training*

## Approach
The research compares virtual room acoustics methods used for synthetic dataset generation in SpatialScaper: the Image Source Method (ISM), Scattering Delay Networks (SDN), and hybrid reverberation approaches.

## Technical reading
The focus is the relationship between room simulation, synthetic training data, and sound event localization and detection. Detailed experimental settings, evaluation metrics and diagrams will be added from the thesis.
